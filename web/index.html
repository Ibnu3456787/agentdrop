Claim your AgentHaven Pass

One Pass per wallet. The first 1,111 Passes are free (gas only); after that each claim costs 0.00005 BNB (+ gas). The Pass is an ERC-721 NFT with fully on-chain art showing your .ai name and Pass number. Claiming sends a single claim() transaction to the Pass contract — this site reads the current price on-chain first and sends exactly that amount, and your wallet shows you exactly what you are signing before you confirm. Honest notes

  • The first 1,111 Passes are free (gas only). Later claims cost 0.00005 BNB each — the contract requires the exact price, so overpaying is impossible and no excess BNB can be locked in.
  • Claim fees accumulate in the contract; only the owner can withdraw them (full balance, no partial withdrawals). The owner cannot change the price — the free tier and the 0.00005 BNB price are fixed in the contract.
  • There is no token and no promised return. The Pass is a collectible badge for Agent ID holders — nothing more.
  • AgentHaven is an independent community project, built for the Agent ID Hackathon. It is unaffiliated with Binance and SPACE ID.
  • The Pass contract is open-source and verified on BscScan. Its only owner powers are an emergency pause, withdrawing claim fees, and ownership transfer — documented in the README.
  • v1 note: the first version of the Pass (0xf8118d…aC5D) was an all-free pilot drop. Its passes remain valid on-chain.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with any website, including this one — this site never asks for them.
