Honest notes

  • Listed Passes are escrowed in the market contract — the seller cannot sell the same Pass twice, and a buyer always receives the Pass they paid for.
  • The marketplace fee is 2.5% of the sale price, taken from the seller's proceeds. The fee can be changed by the contract owner, but it is hard-capped at 10% in the contract and can never go above that.
  • USDT buys are two steps: first approve exactly the listed USDT amount (no unlimited approvals), then buy. Your wallet shows you both transactions before you confirm them.
  • BNB buys are one step: you send exactly the listed price. Overpaying or underpaying reverts on-chain, so no excess BNB can be locked in.
  • The market contract is open-source and verified on BscScan. Its only owner powers are changing the fee (capped at 10%), returning Passes or tokens sent to it by mistake, and ownership transfer — documented in the README. The owner cannot move escrowed listed Passes or change listing prices.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with any website, including this one — this site never asks for them.
